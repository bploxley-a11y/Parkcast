Its free API is restricted to non-commercial use; company deployment requires a compliant free commercial-use source or an appropriate licence. Perceived forecast is a proxy based on daily weather codes, not Apple Weather data. Earlier forecasts influence the colour only when at least two distinct earlier-day snapshots exist and most were adverse; missing history does not prevent green. Seasonal thresholds are provisional regional assumptions, not statistically calibrated attendance forecasts. Weather icons use hourly WMO forecast conditions, including snow. Snow-depth estimates are not verified road conditions. No attendance percentages are inferred. Madrid access alerts are sourced from an automated GitHub Actions municipal-data feed; unavailable or stale data never implies roads are clear. New coordinates are approximate attraction locations; validate before operational use. Parque Warner Madrid and Parque de Atracciones Madrid use the same Madrid metropolitan reference forecast and prior forecast history to keep weather-impact ratings consistent; park-specific road-access alerts can still differ. Opening calendars are independently sourced and never override weather impact; missing calendar data has UNKNOWN operating status, while retaining normal weather colours.
